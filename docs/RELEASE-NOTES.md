Still 2.6.0 adds the Spaced layout and preset.

- **Spaced:** a wide, letter-spaced weekday such as MONDAY, the full date beneath, and the time between two short rules, such as — 3:56:23 PM —. The date follows your region: SEPTEMBER 28, 2026 in the US and 28 SEPTEMBER 2026 elsewhere.
- Choose the **Spaced** preset on the Theme page, or set **Layout** to Spaced on the Style page to combine it with any font, colour, palette, glow, surface or seconds indicator. Weekday, date, seconds and 12/24-hour settings all apply.

Existing preferences are kept and stay on their current layout until you choose another.

Windows: if Still 2.5 or later is installed, open About and press Check for updates. Otherwise download Still-Setup-win-x64.exe, exit the old Still from its notification-area icon, and run Setup. It installs for your Windows user without administrator access and includes .NET. A portable Still-win-x64.zip is also available.

Linux: download Still-linux-x64.tar.gz, then run: tar -xzf Still-linux-x64.tar.gz && cd Still-linux-x64 && ./install.sh

The downloads are unsigned. SHA256SUMS.txt contains the checksums of all three.
