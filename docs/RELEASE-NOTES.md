Still 2.7.0 adds a split-flap Flip layout with dark and light presets.

- **Flip** and **Flip Light:** each digit sits on its own tile with a split line and hinge tabs, inside a recessed tray. The weekday shows in amber with the date beside it. Flip is charcoal; Flip Light is warm white.
- A small pin appears in the corner when **Always on top** is on. In 12-hour time, AM/PM shows next to the date. Turn on seconds for two more tiles.
- Set **Layout** to Flip on the Style page to use it with any colours. The tray, tiles and hinges take their shades from the surface tint, and the digits use the text colour.

Existing preferences are kept and stay on their current layout until you choose another.

Windows: if Still 2.5 or later is installed, open About and press Check for updates. Otherwise download Still-Setup-win-x64.exe, exit the old Still from its notification-area icon, and run Setup.

Linux: download Still-linux-x64.tar.gz, then run: tar -xzf Still-linux-x64.tar.gz && cd Still-linux-x64 && ./install.sh

The downloads are unsigned. SHA256SUMS.txt contains the checksums of all three.
